I'm a software engineer with **4+ years of full-stack experience**, specializing in **PHP and Laravel**. I work across the full delivery cycle, from understanding the problem and designing a solution to deployment and ongoing production support.

I care about building software that solves the right problem and stays easy to maintain as it grows.

- **Products & integrations:** APIs, ERP systems, e-commerce platforms, payment gateways, and external services.
- **System evolution:** legacy modernization, refactoring, database optimization, and application performance.
- **Collaboration:** technical planning, code reviews, and direct communication with clients and product teams.

I'm also expanding my work in **applied AI**, studying **LLMs, RAG, and agents** and exploring their use in practical products and automations.
